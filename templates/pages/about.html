How we think about it

  • Start small. A short session you finish beats a perfect plan you skip.
  • Be honest. You don't need expensive equipment, and we'd rather suggest what fits your goal and budget than sell you everything.
  • Respect your pace. Rest days and restarts are part of the journey.
  • No invented numbers. Streaks, charts and achievements come from real sessions you log.

Good to know

The AI Coach is a rule-based recommendation engine and isn't connected to an external AI model. Workout plans are general guidance, not medical advice or a guarantee of results. If you have a health condition, feel pain or are unsure, talk to a doctor or qualified professional.
