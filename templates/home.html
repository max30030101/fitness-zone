FAQ

Questions, answered

Is {{ site_name }} free to join?

Yes. Creating an account and building workout plans is free. Premium options may come later.

Is the AI Coach a real AI?

Not yet. It's a rule-based engine that builds a plan from your goal, level, equipment and schedule. It isn't connected to an external AI model.

Do I need equipment to start?

No. There are bodyweight workouts for every level. Add equipment later if it helps your goals.

How do payment and delivery work?

Orders are paid by cash on delivery for now. Delivery is free on orders over ₹999 and ₹79 below that. Online payments aren't available yet.

Are the workouts medical advice?

No. They're general guidance. If you have a health condition or feel pain, speak to a doctor or qualified professional first.
